PROTOCOL OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tenbin Labs — tokenization protocol for FX, commodities, and precious metals.
Built for institutional-scale liquidity, predictable redemption, on-chain
settlement. Users swap, stake, and earn yield. Staked assets can be used as
margin collateral. Raised ~$7M led by Galaxy Ventures.
Chain: Ethereum (mainnet).
App: https://app.tenbinlabs.xyz/explore
Docs: https://docs.tenbinlabs.xyz/
Yield partners: Morpho, Euler, Accountable.

Three tokenized assets, each with identical module architecture:
  - tGLD  = Tenbin Gold
  - tMXN  = Tenbin Mexican Peso
  - tBRL  = Tenbin Brazilian Real

Controller = where mint & redeem transactions are sent.
