PROTOCOL OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tenor Finance — non-custodial FIXED-RATE, FIXED-TERM lending/borrowing, on Base.
Built ON TOP OF Morpho (Morpho Blue + Morpho Midnight fixed-rate layer).
Seed round led by Variant. MORPHO rewards active for lenders. Has an OTC section
for long-tail / custom-term markets.
App:  https://app.tenor.finance/markets
Docs: https://docs.tenor.finance/  (addresses page confirmed UP TO DATE by founder)
Explorer (whole Midnight, built by Tenor): https://midnightscan.org/

ARCHITECTURE / KEY INSIGHT
- Tenor markets ARE Morpho market IDs. Filtering by market ID alone would ALSO
  capture other curators (Steakhouse, Gauntlet, etc.) in the same Morpho market.
- To ISOLATE Tenor activity: key on Tenor's OWN routing contracts, not market ID.
- FOUNDER CONFIRMED (TG): lend & borrow actions route via the TENOR ADAPTER
  contract, via BUNDLER3. Tenor router shows on midnightscan.org/activity;
  bundler3 on /mempool.
- Morpho Midnight is Morpho's fixed-rate protocol layer; NOT exclusive to Tenor
  (anyone can build on it). MidnightScan totals (~$3M deposits / ~$2M borrows)
  = ALL of Midnight, not Tenor alone. Tenor is a subset -> good sanity ceiling.

              substr(json_extract_scalar(offer,'$.group'),1,12)='0x74656e6f72'.
              ~1259 Tenor takes of 3121 total (~40% of Midnight take-flow).
              reduceOnly=true = position reductions (only 28) -> exclude for opens.
