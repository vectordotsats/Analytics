--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE ONE IDEA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Coinbase put real US stocks on-chain (Base) as tokens. Morpho lets you post them
as collateral and borrow USDC. So: "borrow dollars against your Apple/Nvidia
shares, on-chain, 24/7." Marketed as variable AND fixed rate. Reality so far:
tiny, variable-only in practice, fixed-rate layer is a GHOST TOWN. That gap =
our lane.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE ASSET (Coinbase tokenized stocks)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Live on BASE since 2026-08-24. Issuer: Coinbase, "B20" token standard.
- Each token = 1:1 claim on a REAL share held in custody by ALPACA (broker,
  ADGM-regulated). Bankruptcy-remote. Reflects dividends + splits.
- Transferable with NO whitelist once minted; trades 24/7 on Aerodrome etc.
- NON-US users only (regulatory gate).
- PRICING: Chainlink feeds (continuous). Morpho uses a Chainlink V2 oracle
  adapter to price the collateral. Oracle = the critical risk (stocks trade
  24/7 on-chain but the underlying equity market has hours -> gap/stale risk).
- Market size: ~$11.4M total stock tokens on Base (early, growing). Ticker list
  doubled to ~10 on Sep 4; more being added.

3. THE ANGLES (our lane -- outcomes + is-it-real)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   fixed is empty. Why? (Leads straight into the Midnight P2P deep-dive.)

   press-release product? <1% utilization of available stock tokens.
D. LIQUIDATION / ORACLE RISK: stocks tokens trade 24/7, underlying market has
   hours. Weekend/after-hours gaps -> liquidation spikes. Track liquidations vs
   equity market open/close. Nobody charts this.

   are they real users or team/MM seeding.
