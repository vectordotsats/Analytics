--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ARC — what it is (mainnet live 2026-09-16)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Layer 1 for financial markets, real-time money movement, agentic (AI) payments.
- GAS PAID IN USDC. No volatile native token to hold. An ARC token exists (10B
  minted) but it's for security/utility/governance only -- fees stay in USDC.
- Sub-second, deterministic finality. Opt-in confidential transactions (WIP).
- Native USDC + EURC + tokenized RWAs (BlackRock BUIDL, Circle USYC).
- StableFX: 24/7 programmable on-chain FX (USDC<->EURC), near-instant settlement.
- Validators are PERMISSIONED, Circle-run set: BlackRock, DTCC, Galaxy, ICE,
  Mastercard, MoneyGram, SBI, Standard Chartered, Sumitomo, Visa, Worldpay.
- 100+ apps at launch. DeFi live: Aave (new V4 market), Morpho, Uniswap, Aero, fomo.
READ IT AS: Circle building the rail where institutions settle stablecoins + RWAs.
Deposits are likely to skew INSTITUTIONAL, not retail -- shapes every metric.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MORPHO x ARC — the integration
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Morpho is "live on Arc from day one as its credit infrastructure" = the chain's
lending/borrowing layer. Context: Morpho ~$2.8B USDC deposits overall (Jul 2026);
on Base ~$1.4B active USDC loans + $500M deposits via Coinbase Lend (the template).

HOW EACH SIDE WINS:
  ARC gets   -> instant, credible DeFi credit (earn yield / borrow against assets)
                without Circle building a lender. Makes the chain useful on day 1.
  MORPHO gets-> firehose of institutional USDC/EURC + RWA collateral on a chain
                Circle funnels partners into. New TVL surface, less crowded than
                Base/Ethereum. First-mover on the institutional lane.

LIVE PRODUCTS:
  - Arc Earn Kit    = API for fintechs to embed curated Morpho Vaults in their apps.
  - Pulsar Money Earn = USDC + EURC vaults CURATED BY STEAKHOUSE FINANCIAL.
  - SafePal Earn    = hardware-wallet USDC yield via Morpho.
  - Variable-rate lend/borrow now at app.morpho.org.
COMING:
  - Morpho Midnight = fixed-rate lending on Arc (same tech as Tenor -- we know it).
  - Circle Mint integration = borrow against digital assets e.g. cirBTC.
