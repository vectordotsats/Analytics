================================================================================
METEORA PRIMER — how DAMM / DLMM work, and what that means for analytics
================================================================================
For: Vector. Purpose: understand the machine before charting it, so we DICTATE
what we build instead of forking. Chain: Solana. Updated: 2026-09-14.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE ONE IDEA EVERYTHING ORBITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A pool holds 2 tokens. Traders swap and pay a FEE. Liquidity Providers (LPs)
supply the tokens and collect the fees. The catch = IMPERMANENT LOSS (IL): when
price moves, the pool auto-sells your winner and leaves you the loser, so an LP
can earn big fees and STILL underperform just holding.
  => The only question that matters: FEES EARNED vs IL SUFFERED = did LPs profit?
  Every metric we build is a way of answering that. Everyone charts volume;
  almost nobody charts LP outcomes. That's our lane.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DAMM — Dynamic AMM (the simple one)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Constant product (x*y=k, Uniswap-v2 style). Liquidity spread across ALL prices
  0 -> infinity. Passive: deposit and forget.
- "Dynamic" twists: (a) idle tokens lent out via Meteora VAULTS for extra yield
  on top of swap fees; (b) fee rate can adjust.
- Position = FUNGIBLE LP token = plain % share of the pool.
- Valuing an LP = share x reserves. Easy.
- ANALYTICS: TVL, LP fees, IL all TRACTABLE (you know each LP's share + reserves).
  This is where "do LPs profit" is cleanly answerable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DLMM — Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker (the powerful, hard one)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Concentrated liquidity via discrete BINS. Price axis chopped into bins; each
  bin = a tiny pool at ONE fixed price. Spacing = BIN STEP (e.g. 0.2%).
- Exactly ONE ACTIVE BIN at a time (where trading happens, holds both tokens).
  Bins ABOVE active hold only one token; bins BELOW hold only the other.
- LP chooses WHICH bins to fund and in what SHAPE (spot / curve / bid-ask).
  Concentrate near price -> far more fees per dollar, BUT only while price stays
  in your bins.
- Inside a bin: ZERO slippage (constant sum). Price steps bin-to-bin as trades
  exhaust each bin's liquidity.
- DYNAMIC FEES = base fee + VOLATILITY SURCHARGE that spikes when price whipsaws.
  This is why DLMM prints for LPs on memecoins: more volatile = fatter fees.
- OUT-OF-RANGE RISK: when price leaves your bins you're 100% in one token,
  earning ZERO fees, fully exposed. IL is sharper + path-dependent.
- Positions are individual (NFT-like), NOT fungible -> must ACTIVELY REBALANCE as
  price drifts. That labor is exactly what HAWKFI automates.
- ANALYTICS: volume + fees EASY (swaps). True LP PnL/IL HARD (must reconstruct
  each position's bins + the active bin over time). = the holy-grail-hard metric.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LIFE OF A DLMM POSITION (walk it once, feel it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Say a SOL/MEME pool, price = 100. Bin step 1%. You LP $1,000 concentrated in
bins 99, 100, 101 (tight around price).
  t0 DEPOSIT: active bin=100. Your liquidity split across 99/100/101. You hold a
     mix of SOL + MEME. Earning fees on every swap that crosses your bins.
  t1 IN RANGE, price chops 99<->101: JACKPOT. Every wiggle pays you the base fee
     PLUS the volatility surcharge. Tight concentration = max fees per dollar.
  t2 PRICE PUMPS to 130: it blew past bin 101. As price rose it SOLD all your SOL
     for MEME... wait, inverse: as MEME pumps vs SOL, the pool sold your MEME for
     SOL through each bin. Now you're parked in bins below price = 100% one token,
     OUT OF RANGE, earning ZERO. You also "sold the winner early" = IL.
  t3 REBALANCE: to earn again you must withdraw + redeposit around 130 (new bins).
     Costs gas + realizes the IL. Do it too often = bleed; too late = earn nothing.
     HawkFi automates this decision. (Metric: rebalance frequency, and does auto
     beat manual?)
KEY FEELING: DLMM LPing = a bet that price stays NEAR where you put your bins,
long enough for fees to out-earn the IL from it eventually leaving.

4. SUPPORTING PIECES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- VAULTS (meteora_vault_solana): lending layer DAMM parks idle liquidity in.
  Extra yield source -> "total LP yield = swap fees + vault yield."
- DBC (Dynamic Bonding Curve): memecoin LAUNCHPAD. Token starts on a bonding
  curve, then GRADUATES into a DAMM/DLMM pool at a threshold. Launch/graduation
  analytics live here.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE METRIC ANGLES (our lane — LP outcomes, not volume)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranked easy -> hard:
  A. POOL CREATION / GRADUATION rate (poolcreated, DBC) = memecoin-launch pulse.
  B. LOCKED-LP / rug-resistance (amm_evt_lock) = trust signal. Sleeper, low effort.
  C. LP FEE REVENUE (claimfee + swap fees) = what LPs actually earned. Tractable.
  D. TIME-IN-RANGE vs OUT-OF-RANGE (DLMM) = HERO METRIC. Needs liquidity bins +
     active bin over time. Medium effort, high signal, nobody shows it.
  E. DYNAMIC-FEE CAPTURE (DLMM) = fees earned per $ of liquidity in volatile vs
     calm windows. Shows the memecoin edge.
  F. BIN CONCENTRATION = how tight LPs cluster; risk appetite.
  G. REBALANCE FREQUENCY + HAWKFI EDGE = auto vs manual outcomes. Differentiator.
  H. FULL LP PnL / IL (DLMM) = holy grail, hardest. Build toward it, don't open on it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT MAKES THIS EASIER (discipline, so we don't wander)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ONE hero metric first, not the buffet. Rec: TIME-IN-RANGE (D). Sharp + doable.
2. Start POOL-LEVEL, not position-level. Per-NFT tracking is the hard mode; add
   it later once pool-level works.
3. Reference pools: pick 2-3 known DLMM pools (a hot memecoin + a stable pair) to
   test + validate against Meteora's own app. Never fly blind.
4. Confirm columns before SQL: pull meteora_version_dlmm table columns; find how
   bins are stored (bin id, price, liquidity) + how active bin is derivable.
5. Always scope by pool + time (Solana = billions of rows).
6. Define the exact question each metric answers BEFORE writing SQL. Label
   precisely, validate vs ground truth. (Agua lesson: don't ship what you can't
   validate.)
