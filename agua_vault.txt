--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTOCOL OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Agua Vault = "Opportunity Vault" by Tropical Water Management Corp, a higher-yield
product within the Reservoir ecosystem (separate from core rUSD).
Mechanic: Deposit USDC -> receive yield-bearing vault shares that compound every
second (ERC-4626-style). Vault holds ACTUAL USDC (unlike wsrUSD/trUSD which
mint/burn rUSD). Shares are a LayerZero OFT (bridgeable, yield syncs cross-chain).
Withdrawals: fee-free after lockup; early exit pays a small fee. Early-exit fees
accumulate in the vault as UNTRACKED SURPLUS USDC -> effectively extra yield to
remaining/patient depositors.
Docs: https://docs.tropicalwater.xyz/

ANALYSIS PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Foundation (build first):
  1. Deposit activity & depositors — daily deposits/withdrawals, unique
     depositors, new vs returning, TVL over time (assets under mgmt).
Then:
  2. Total yield paid out — realized APY from share price (convertToAssets) growth.
  3. Fees generated — early-exit fees.
Sharp / differentiated angles ("see what others don't"):
  - EARLY-EXIT FEE = HIDDEN YIELD to stayers. Docs: fees accrue as untracked
    surplus USDC. Quantify total + effective APY boost to lockup-completers.
  - LOCKUP COHORT / UNLOCK LADDER — deposits grouped by lockup-end -> future
    withdrawal-pressure view.
  - STICKY vs MERCENARY — % exiting early (paying fee) vs waiting; retention by cohort.
  - WHALE CONCENTRATION — deposit-size distribution, top-10 share.
  - CAPITAL SOURCE — is deposited USDC fresh, from CEXs, or cannibalized from
    other Reservoir products (rUSD/wsrUSD)?
  - CROSS-CHAIN SPLIT — share supply bridged to Monad vs held on ETH (OFT).

IMPORTANT ANALYSIS NOTES:
  - TVL = totalAssets (AUM), NOT USDC balance of vault — allocator deploys USDC
    out to earn yield, so raw USDC-in-vault understates TVL. Use totalAssets /
    convertToAssets(totalSupply), or share supply x share price.
  - User deposits/withdrawals come from the vault's Deposit/Withdraw EVENTS, not
    raw USDC transfers (raw transfers conflate user flows with allocator moves).
  - Share price (convertToAssets) growth = realized yield (same pattern as
    Reservoir wsrUSD savingcoinv2_call_converttoassets).
