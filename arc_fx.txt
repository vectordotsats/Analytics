--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE FINDING (why DEX, not StableFX)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Circle StableFX (RFQ = Request for Quote; MM quotes off-chain, only the trade
settles on-chain via PvP escrow) is API-abstracted and NASCENT — no dominant
on-chain settlement contract shows up in EURC flow yet. So real, trackable
on-chain FX on Arc today = DEX pools (Aerodrome concentrated-liquidity = "CLPool").
=> Build FX off the Aero CL pools now. Keep StableFX on WATCH (re-poll maturity).

Evidence (Bitquery arc, EURC receivers): biggest EURC venue = CLPool
0xbe080ac37ad1305dfcc9521f5e6f68cfdc41b7fa (EUR88M, 9270 swaps). Also a Safe
0x19cbc83ded190616d819555c3b42b3b3507fc41e (EUR85M, 548 large txns = MM/OTC desk).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT WE TRACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FX volume over time (daily + cumulative), EURC<->USDC.
2. Net direction: USDC->EURC vs EURC->USDC (euro vs dollar demand).
3. Swap count + avg size (retail vs institutional).
4. Per-pool / per-pair split (widen to other stablecoin pairs as they list).
v2: pool liquidity/TVL (Mint/Burn), on-chain FX rate vs off-chain EUR/USD.
