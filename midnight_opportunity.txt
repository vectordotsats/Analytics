1. WHAT CHANGED (state now, Nov 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Midnight = Morpho's fixed-rate, fixed-term credit protocol. Offer-based model:
  a lender keeps funds earning variable on Morpho Blue until their fixed-rate
  OFFER is TAKEN (the "Take" = borrow). This "offered capital" design is the fix
  for the thin-liquidity problem that killed earlier fixed-rate attempts.
- Live on BASE 2026-07-21; expanded to ETHEREUM 2026-09-08 (USDC backed by WBTC /
  cbBTC). Still a gradual, security-paced rollout (new features weekly).
- Adoption inflected HARD. Early weeks were ~$908k TVL / ~$152k active loans. The
  trackers now show a steep climb (a recent DefiLlama-sourced read ~$56M TVL,
  +~557% in a week; an older cached DefiLlama page still read ~$4.3M / +372% 30d).
  *** The exact live number is UNCONFIRMED — pinning it on-chain is our Step 1. ***
- ~$5B sitting in Morpho Vaults still CAN'T enter Midnight pending a DAO action ->
  when that unlocks, big inflow potential. Watch governance.
- "Several unidentified enterprises/institutions building on Midnight in beta,
  announcements expected" -> those are future Tenor-shaped clients.
- Governance active: MIP to add new LLTVs on Midnight (forum, 2026-11-04).
- Fees small but real (~$29k/30d per DefiLlama). Next MORPHO unlock 2026-11-20.

materialize small daily tables; label precisely (TVL vs active loans vs util);
validate every number vs app.morpho.org / MidnightScan / DefiLlama before shipping.

BUILD LOG — SUPPLY/LOAN LEG + DEPOSITS RECONCILIATION (2026-11-10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dune's decoded midnight_evt_take is BROKEN (view references decoded.args_17 -> the
  whole view fails to compile). Do NOT use evt_take. RAW-DECODE from logs instead.
- RAW Take decode (base.logs / ethereum.logs), topic0
  0xbd88656d0ee14d32d4f74f814f6ccb5c750a4c542d6fdf83ee00ddca4edebff0:
    topic1 = market id; data word3 = group (Tenor isolation key);
    word6 = units (FACE); word12 = totalUnitsDelta (int256, signed).
  ratifierData is the ONLY dynamic field (appended at tail), so head word offsets are
  fixed/safe. Decode: bytearray_to_int256(bytearray_substring(data, 12*32+1, 32)).
- Contracts: Base Midnight 0xadedd8ab6de832766fedf0fac4992e5c4d3ea18a ;
  ETH Midnight 0x471686c42792F93528B000beF54bC10E3aa2045f.
- Loan token from market JSON: json_extract_scalar(market,'$.loanToken') = USDC (6dp)
  on both chains. Collateral token comes off the collateral events (prior log).
- marketstate VIEW (midnight_call_marketstate) is USELESS for aggregates: coverage
  2/1302 markets (Base), 0/1610 (ETH). Lesson: Dune call_* view tables are sparse;
  reconstruct from logs, never rely on view-call coverage.
- units = FACE (principal + full-term interest). Sum(totalUnitsDelta) per market =
  net outstanding in FACE units.
- RESULT (2026-11-10): Base $12.55M + ETH $86.42M = $98.97M net outstanding (face).
  Brackets the Morpho app correctly: Outstanding loans $92.07M (present value < face)
  and Loans originated $102.79M (cumulative > net). Internally consistent -> the
  reconstruction is validated as the LOAN book.
- RECONCILIATION CORRECTION: Morpho "Total deposits" (~$285M) is NOT collateral+supply.
  It = supply-side liquidity = available Borrow liquidity + Outstanding loans. The
  available-liquidity part is lenders' OFFERED capital = largely OFF-CHAIN signed limit
  orders on Morpho Blue -> NOT in on-chain events. So the full $285M is NOT
  reconstructable on-chain alone (needs Morpho API/orderbook). Collateral is likely NOT
  part of "Total deposits".
- CLEAN ON-CHAIN METRICS to build on instead: (1) Collateral present (done), (2)
  Outstanding loans (this reconstruction; refine face->present-value to match $92.07M).
  Tenor-share story = Tenor's share of Outstanding loans + share of Collateral.
